Common questions

How do I invite a friend to an event? Add them first: in the sidebar, enter their @handle and tap Add; once they accept, drag an event onto their name (on phones, drag it and drop it on their picture at the bottom), or press and hold an event and choose “Invite a friend”.

How do I share a whole calendar? Drag the calendar from the sidebar onto a friend.

How do I delete my account? Settings → Account → Delete my account.

Can I use it without signing in? Yes — “Continue in demo mode” keeps everything on your device only, with no syncing or sharing.
